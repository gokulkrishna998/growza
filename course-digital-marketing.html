Flagship learning pathway

Mastery in Digital Marketing.

Understand the complete digital marketing ecosystem—from search visibility and paid media to analytics, content and conversion.

Program overview

Build a connected marketing skill set.

This program is designed for learners who want to understand how digital channels work together. Explore the strategy behind each channel, practice with industry tools and learn how to evaluate campaign performance.

What you will explore

  • Marketing fundamentals, customer journeys and digital strategy
  • Keyword research, on-page, off-page and technical SEO
  • Search intent, topical authority, AEO and GEO foundations
  • Google Ads and Meta Ads campaign planning
  • GA4, Google Tag Manager and Search Console
  • Content strategy, social media and email marketing
  • Landing pages, CRO, lead generation and reporting
  • AI-assisted workflows, research and responsible use

Practical learning outcomes

By the end of the learning journey, you should be better equipped to plan a digital strategy, conduct an SEO audit, structure an ad campaign, interpret analytics and present a practical marketing plan. Outcomes depend on your participation and project work.

Who is this for?

Students, career starters, working professionals, entrepreneurs and business owners who want a structured understanding of digital marketing.

Tools and platforms

Google Ads, Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager, Google Search Console, Meta Ads Manager, WordPress and selected AI productivity tools.
